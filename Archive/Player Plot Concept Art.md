# Player Plot Concept Art

**Latest Japanese-setting exploration:** [[Archive/Art/Japanese Village Concept Art|Japanese Village Concept Art]]. Images below preserve the earlier visual direction.

Concept images for **Sell Swords!**, based on [[Design/Game Design Document|Game Design Document]], [[Design/Decisions Log|Decisions Log]], [[Sessions/2026-10-17]], and the saved visual references. These are visual explorations rather than implemented game changes.

The current Studio scene was not accessible in this chat. The existing forge, anvil, Hilt Bench, shared three-slot rack, and NPC behavior were reviewed through the vault's build notes.

## Current direction — spacious plot (v2)

![[References/Concept Art/player-plot-spacious-v2.png]]

**Allen's direction:** the plot should be larger, like the Create Pottery! references, so players have room to buy upgrades and the stations do not feel cluttered. This supersedes the compact layout below.

The revised concept separates a modest crafting yard from the shop, with a broad central approach, generous open lawn, and three empty foundation pads suggesting future purchases. The land stays spacious with only the early stations installed. Stations and paths can extend into this reserve as players progress; exact plot dimensions and pad placement remain exploratory.

## Earlier compact plot (v1)

![[References/Concept Art/player-plot-v1.png]]

A warm timber-and-stone village smithy with an attached open workshop and a road-facing shop. The workshop exposes the crafting activities so the plot looks active from outside. The retail side uses a wider browsing area and visible sword silhouettes.

- **Crafting:** ore roller → forge → anvil → Hilt Bench, grouped within a short walk.
- **Selling:** individual sword holders with a clear empty/occupied state; an empty holder shows the proposed **+** interaction.
- **Shop:** an open frontage, simple counter, and shared materials tie the retail space to the workshop.
- **Growth:** extra holders, heating slots, and a larger display rack can extend the same visual kit.

The counter is a furnishing in this proposal; a staffed cashier remains a later upgrade from the GDD. The two heating slots illustrate an upgraded forge. This is a compact starter-to-midgame direction, not a fixed starting inventory.

## Stations and holders

![[References/Concept Art/station-kit-v1.png]]

The sheet isolates the forge, stump-mounted anvil, Hilt Bench, ore roller, single-sword holder, and three-sword rack.

The holders present each sword's broad face, grip up and blade down, so the shape and material remain visible. Each has a small front price placard. Separate pedestals make the holder-count upgrade easy to understand; a combined rack is an alternative footprint.

For a Roblox build, simplify the illustrated grain, stone variation, foliage, and hardware. Preserve the large silhouettes, contrasting materials, clear interaction surfaces, and walking space. Heating trays should visibly enter the heated chamber when modeled.
