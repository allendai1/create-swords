# Blade Shaping Study

Historical review retained for reference. Current map and station layout: [[Art/Town Street Redesign References]], [[Art/Workshop Layout]] and [[Current Status]]. Earlier dimensions, tasks and proposed rules below are not the active roadmap.

![[References/Blade Shaping/blade-shaping-study-v1.png]]

First visual exploration of tantō, wakizashi, and katana progressing from a universal blank through lengthening, taper/curve, tip formation at 90%, and optional refinement to 100%. The lower strip explores hammer contact and automatic repositioning without tongs.

## Review notes

- This is a visual concept, not approved geometry or a frame-accurate animation specification.
- The generated wakizashi and katana are too similar in length. A later model/reference pass must establish distinct proportions at a shared scale; do not measure dimensions from this sheet.
- The image begins curving/forming tips earlier than the proposed stage labels imply. In the animation, make each stage's contribution clearer.
- The impact closeup exaggerates the dent. Use a restrained, quick local deformation in motion, without leaving a deep notch or introducing wobble.
- The orange-to-gray presentation illustrates hot versus refined steel; it does not add a cooling timer or change the agreed material roster.
