names: 
Create swords!
Craft Swords!
Sell Swords!
------------------------
Idea: Roblox where players create and sell swords in their shop. They manually shape the sword, like in the game Create Pottery. They sell to NPCs for money. 

NPCs could be different fictional characters, for example maybe a ninja might come in and want a nice rainbow katana. maybe a knight will come in and want a shiny longsword.

Swords can be molded from steel or whatever material for more value, add finishes to it to make it more valuable. They can polish it, fire it.

They put the sword they create into their shop.

NPCs come in, examine it, offer a price, and you accept or decline.

Declining can sometimes make the NPC offer to pay more.

In the game Create Pottery! players go to a level to "Reroll clay" which generates a clay mold with different textures which signify the rarity. For example it might generate a Blueberry Clay, which has a rarity of "Rare"

the game also has an iphone players can use where npcs text you asking for a sword like "My bowl is cracked, can you make me this bowl with a picture of the bowl" and they might offer money or special currency gems for it.

Players can Use gems to buy upgrades like 
- more customers
- more special customers,
- Clients - more clients
- regular clients - clients text sooner
in the clay game, after you've molded the clay, you can glaze it for +10%, put it into kiln to fire it which takes some amount of time, then put in on display for sale

You can buy new cup or bowl shapes that sell for more. 

You can buy more space, that gives you another re-roll space, meaning now you get 2 different clay rarities at the same time

could buy a cashier upgrade, so player doesnt need to manually accept the offer

can buy a person to automate creating pottery

our game could start in a small village, maybe 6 houses

start with the core game which is first creating or generating a block with some material for example steel a common rarity. take that piece of steel which is in rectangle form. take it to the anvil. popup the ui on the left that shows the finished shape, and the current shape overlayed onto it. then in front of you is your sword in progress. you have to hit the block with your hammer on the sides to indent it so that the silhouette of yours matches the silhouette of the finished sword. there's a bar that tells you how close you are. you can choose to get perfect score or finish at 80%. see [References/clay silhouette goal.png] for example of this.

after this poc is done, the rest of the game is basically a shop tycoon style game.


concepts for actually shaping the sword : 
* the user first has to heat the metal in a forge. then it transfers to the anvil where they have to hammer it until it is bigger than the sword silhouette guide. at this point, the metal would still keep its rectangular shape. then, the user can switch to a cutting tool where they can trim off the excess in order to shape the sword. this is done by having the user click and drag to trace a guideline. 
* after the user heats the metal in the forge and transfers to the anvil, they just need to click on the metal 8 times. for example, each click would slowly shape the metal into the sword shape.
* after the user heats the metal in the forge and transfers to the anvil, they have a total of 10 seconds to complete the shaping process as the metal cools down. they need to finish before it's too stiff to shape. this shaping process occurs by having specific points display on the metal one by one. The user must click each point as it appears within 2 seconds. the points appear as a small circle and decrease in size as their time runs out, similar to the game OSU.

The start to finish loop core-loop

You get a random block of material from some generator, for example a basic iron block.

You put the iron block into the fire. Wait 15 seconds

Take the iron block from the fire. 

Walk to Anvil. 

Select what block to use. Then what shape for the sword. Each sword shape has a certain value that it will sell for. For example, the basic dagger might sell for 50.
Then, start making the sword.

When sword is finished, take it over to the bench.
At the bench, select a hilt. A hilt can add some additional value to your sword.
Maybe a separate interactive step to attach the hilt to the sword? (need to think of some way to do this)

Once blade and hilt are attached, sword is complete.
Sword is now in your toolbar. You can have 8 swords in your toolbar. You also have a backpack. You can store more in your backpack and moves things into your backpack from the toolbar as well.

You then go to your shop that has sword holders. A store has a set amount of sword holders, and you can upgrade the amount that you have so that your store has a larger inventory to sell. For each sword holder, you can press a + button on it, and choose which sword to sell from your toolbar/backpack. Once you select your sword to sell, it then goes onto the sword holder display, and the sword is for sale. 

NPCs can go up to it, browse the store, and ultimately offer money for it.
