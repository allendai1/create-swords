
* Players should feel a sense of urgency

* when i was playing the pottery game, it always felt like there's something more I could be doing. While I'm waiting for the kiln to finish my pot, I can work on creating more pots. After creating my pot, I was ready to take the pot out of the kiln and replace it with one in my inventory that needs to be heated. with the finished pottery, i need to put it in the shop so that my shop isn't empty and customers have something to buy. after the shop, I need to go back to make sure my worker has enough clay on the shelf for him to take from and crate more, which means i need to roll more pots. i also needed to manage special requests from customers, who wanted say a cup with a specific material. i needed to custom make that and deliver it through the mobile phone gui. And with deliveries, i needed a bulk of 6 potteries, so while the delivery is going out, i can start preparing the next delivery. i feel like i did a lot just so I could eventually get to one of the employee upgrades that automates this for me. so when i first got the potter employee who could shape and glaze a pot and then put in on the shelf, it automated 1 part of the loop. then i had to take it from the shelf, and fire it myself, and then put it in the shop. once i got the kiln operator. i could then do less kiln operating myself. there's still the option do to it myself since there's always 1 more station than there is an employee. It should feel like the player is operating a large business, because they are.
* random events can add to this - like sometimes there'll be a "rush hour" that does 2x customer rate for like 3 minutes, or a ore rarity chance increaser than increases the chance of a rare roll. these events give players a sense of urgency to make more pots to take advantage of the increased cash flow or customer flow.
* there's random VIP customers, that are highlighted when they enter the shop that catches players attentions. 
* The fact that you need to be in the shop and closer to the customer's price causes a sense of urgency. the player will check the shop more to make sure they're not missing a sale.
