### What changed in the game

- **No "Iron ingot collected"** when you take ore off the box, and **no "Ingot loaded"** when you put one in a furnace. `ForgeService`, `ForgeController`.
- **A new metal is a small toast**, "New metal: X" (the picker's toast, bottom of the screen), instead of the reward card. NEW on the ore lid stays. `RewardCard`.
- **Every rack slot gets its marker (ingot shelf and sword racks).** This was the root cause of both screenshots. The place streams parts in as you get near, and `RackController` built one marker per slot once, at start, from the slots that had arrived by then. Slots still streaming in never got a marker: the left shelf showed 4 of 6, the right 1 of 6, each sword rack 3 of 6. The four racks (`IngotRack`, `IngotRack2`, `SwordRack`, `SwordRack2`) now stream in whole (`ModelStreamingMode = Atomic`), so every slot is there when the markers are built.
- **No prompts through walls.** Every prompt has `RequiresLineOfSight` off, since its own station's parts would block Roblox's check. `PromptFilter` now looks from your head to each nearby prompt's part 4×/s. If a wall is in the way, the prompt is out of reach (its `MaxActivationDistance` set to 0 on your screen) until you can see it. What doesn't block: the prompt's own station model, characters/NPCs, and invisible or non-colliding parts. Checked in Edit from 16 spots around every prompt on the plot: each is still reachable from its front, and walls block it from behind.
- **The Employees window's dark veil reaches the top of the screen.** `CrewGui` now ignores the top-bar inset, like the Mail and Deliveries windows.
- **Both anvils face the same way.** Anvil 2 was turned 180°. The stone mat under them and the crafter's standing spot (room side) stay where they were.
- **Anvil 2 is a second anvil.** Its `CrafterPrompt` became a `ForgePrompt`, and `AnvilController` works whichever anvil's prompt you use. The crafter waits while you stand at Anvil 2, then claims it from when he heads there until he's done hammering: its prompt goes off and "In use" shows over it. **The crew window opens only from the Employee button.** E at Anvil 2 forges, and Etching Table 2 lost its prompt (it opened the Etcher's page).
- **Finishing a blade leaves the anvil straight away.** The 2.5 s "Bronze Tantō! 60 coins" pause is gone; a failed save still says so.
- **Mail: Hand in hands in.** No picker: it gives the cheapest of your swords that fits, for letters and orders alike. With none that fits it shakes, as before.
- **Employees max at Lv. 5.** Levels at 0 / 10 / 30 / 70 / 150 swords made (about 12 min, 40 min, 1.5 h and 3 h of the crafter working at roughly a sword every 75 s). The staff need 2.5× as many jobs (`STAFF_SCALE`). The crafter's quality rises 8% a level (70% → 100% at Lv. 5).
- **Lucky Roller I–III removed.** Luck is only the merges' ×(1 + 0.5 × merges). The three pads are gone.
- **Furnace 2 comes after Faster Heating III.** Anvil 2 still needs Furnace 2, so the crafter comes later too.
- **Pad labels:** one smaller line, "Katana Sword · 400", shown only within 14 studs of you (`TycoonController`). Far pads are just the green pad.
- **Pads moved apart:**
  - The blueprint pad (Wakizashi → Gladius) moved from beside the anvil and furnace to the stone circle in the middle of the workshop.
  - The Ore Slot / Merge pad moved 5 studs right, onto the Lucky Roller's old spot, so the lever can stand in the middle.
  - Furnace 2's pad never shows with Faster Heating's any more.
- **A big lever in the middle of the ore roller.** The lever is 1.6× bigger, with a thick arm (0.55) and a bigger red knob. It stands front and centre of the roller, in front of the middle stand, with its base 0.04 into the floor. The prompt's part sits on the knob and no longer collides; the roller's pivot is unchanged.
- **Tutorial spotlight.** A step done by pressing a button (Open your mail, Hand it in, the Gem Shop) dims the screen around that button with an amber ring pulsing on it. Taps go through. `StarterGui.SpotlightGui`; the guide line stays bright over it.
- **Gem Shop step.** After the tutorial, once you have gems (the daily reward), the guide says "Open the Gem Shop" and spotlights the House button. With the shop open, it says "Gems buy boosts for your shop that last" over the upgrades. It's shown once: opening the shop is saved as funnel step 11, "Opened the Gem Shop" (`GemService` "tour").
- **Shaping prototype:**
  - **View:** the blade and anvil fill the screen, with a thin bar on top (side, match, legend). The face-on diagram is gone. The target outline glows on the blade itself, on both faces, turning with it, and the steel is tinted red where too wide and blue where too narrow.
  - **Sand** (from a 90% match) swaps the hammer for a sanding block: hold the mouse or a finger on the blade and drag. It takes 0.5 cm/s off under the block (±4% of the blade), never past the line. **Hammer** swaps back.
  - `Rules.smooth` became `Rules.sand`.
