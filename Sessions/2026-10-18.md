**Anvil friction review → Hot spots mode**
- Allen asked for the lowest-friction way to shape. Review: Silhouette needs 130–182 bot hits per Masterwork and precise taps; Trace fights the finger on phones; Clicks has no skill; Points is low-friction but the hits don't feel tied to the shape. Other ideas noted: swipe strokes, press-and-hold flow (closest to Create Pottery!), one-line trace, auto-swinging hammer, and "skill is a bonus, not a gate" for every mode.
- Built **Hot spots** (concept 4, details in the [[Create Swords! GDD]] §5) as `Modes/HotSpots` with shared rules in `ShapingModes` and a server check in `FinishCheck`. It's now the anvil's default mode (M still switches).
- AnvilSelfCheck has new Hot spots tests (all Perfect = exact target at Masterwork, one Good = Masterwork, two = Superior, all misses = Crude with the blank untouched, junk refused, timing tiers) and passes.
- Playtest: Stone ingot → Dagger → 10 Space strikes → 10/10, 6 Perfect, 4 Good → "Finish · Fine", no errors. The spot and ring show on the blade edge.

**Shape card silhouettes**
- Allen: the silhouettes look off, especially the Katana's top. Found: the cards drew only the 40 slice widths and skipped the extra half-slice the 3D blade and anvil panel add at each end, so tips came out cut short. Cards now use the same outline as the 3D blade (`BladeMesh.stationX` / `stationWidth`).
- The Katana data itself is the bigger issue: it was traced from the Creator Store model "Sword katana" (Haantoca) by raycasting, widened ×2.15 to be playable, and the raycast left a zig-zag along the spine (alternating widths) and a blunt nub at the tip. Allen said yes to redrawing it (below).
- **Katana redrawn** (`Blueprints.katana()`): widths from real proportions, about 1.7× real width; spine arcs smoothly (`-0.7·(1-t)²`) from the hilt to a point on the axis; width 0.78 → 0.55, then the edge sweeps up to the point over the last 10% (kissaki). The blade's axis runs from the hilt to the point (that's what lets the tip be a real point), so `SwordRenderer` turns held/displayed blades by the root angle (~7.6°) to leave the guard straight. Curve is ~1.9% of the length (a real one is ~2.6%; deeper would push the hilt end outside the blank).
- Checked: AnvilSelfCheck passes (the bot reaches 97.4% on the new Katana, ≥95% needed for ★★★); the shape card shows a smooth curve and a sharp point; a test Katana on a stand leaves the guard straight and curves up to the point.

**Accent lines on the Katana**
- Allen shared a screenshot: the line on a finished Katana looked like a sharpie. Causes: the bevel lines were placed as a fraction of each edge's distance from the blade's axis (off-centre on the redrawn Katana, so one line hugged the edge), they followed every hammer dent, and they were a fixed dark grey.
- `BladeMesh` now places the ridge band (thickest part) across the blade's own width (`blueprint.ridge`, default middle 60%), smoothed over ±2 stations and kept inside the real edges; lines are the blade colour 30% darker and a little thinner. The Katana has `ridge = { from = 0.28, to = 0.38, line = "single" }` (one shinogi line about a third in from the spine) and `yokote = 0.9` (a short line across where the point begins). SwordRenderer sets the colour before drawing the lines.
- Checked on test swords in a playtest: a clean Katana shows a faint shinogi line following the curve plus the yokote; a Katana with simulated hammer dents keeps its rough edges but a smooth ridge; the Shortsword still has its two ridge lines and fuller.
