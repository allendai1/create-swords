## Hilting more satisfying

Allen: "How can we make the hilting process more satisfying". Claude found the drag gives no feedback, the clang comes with a 0.12-stud camera nudge, and the view closes 0.6 s later without showing the finished sword. It suggested four ideas (a reveal, a harder seat, a drag with resistance and a magnet snap, haptics) and kept the drag untimed and unable to fail (Decisions 2026-10-20 and 10-22). Allen: "I like 1,2, and 4". Built in Edit, **not played**.

- **Seat impact:** the clang, then a 0.06 s still, then a camera punch (shake 0.1 + 0.05 × tier studs) and a push-in, dying over 0.3 s. Sparks (`OreRollFx.Sparks`, 8 + 6 × tier) and a flash of light (range 5 + tier) come from where the hilt meets the blade. They are hot orange below Rare and the tier's colour from Rare up. Tier is the metal's rarity, 1 (Common) to 6 (Secret).
- **Reveal:** 0.35 s after the seat the sword lifts (12% of the way to the camera, with a slight overshoot) and turns about its length to face the camera. At 0.8 s a sweep of light (white blended 60% toward the tier colour) runs guard to tip along the edge bevel with `Cues.HiltReveal`, a sword-draw sound. At 1.7 s it drops down the screen and fades, and the view closes at 2.0 s (was 0.6 s). A click, tap or Space / A / R2 after the lift skips to the drop.
- **Why not the white polish glare:** it marks a mirror-polished blade, so the reveal uses the rarity colour and a fresh blade doesn't look polished.
- **Haptics:** `HapticEffect` (phones and gamepads that support it): a light tick every 0.3 studs of slide (about 4 per seat) and a hit on the seat. Nothing on devices without it.
- **Shared:** the glare line moved from `SwordRenderer` to `SwordMeshCache.edgePath` / `.along` so the sweep follows the blade's curve. `SwordRenderer` uses them, and its unused BladeCore require is gone.
- **Checks:** HiltSeat, SwordRenderer and AnvilController compile. A cloned `Shared` loads, and `edgePath` gives 14 points guard to tip on the tanto (1.29-stud blade) and the katana (3.52, following its curve). An Edit render of the revealed pose timed out and was cleaned up, so the angle, timing and sound are **not seen yet**. Sound volume not heard.

**To test in Play:** forge a Common sword and seat it: an orange spark burst, a punch, then the sword lifts, faces you, a sweep runs along it with the draw sound, and it drops away. Seat a Rare-or-better metal: bigger burst and shake in its colour. Tap during the reveal: it skips. On a phone: ticks while sliding, a thump on the seat.

## Faster Strikes

Allen: "how can we improve the manual forging process. seems to take a lot of time at 12 hits per sword. maybe improve the rate at which we can hit through upgrades?" He turned down hold-to-strike ("holding it would still take the same amount of time ... since theres a cooldown inbetween strikes") and fewer, heavier strikes ("would take away the the stages of shaping the sword"), then: "lets do this upgrade. call it Faster Strikes upgrade. what are you thinking for pricing though".

- **What it does:** Faster Strikes I–V speed up your anvil's strike cycle (`Upgrades.STRIKE_PACE` 1, 0.9 … 0.5): the windup, drop, hammer recovery and the blade's slide all scale; the dent keeps its own 0.09 s so the metal never snaps. One strike: 0.75 s → ~0.375 s at V, so 12 strikes ~9 s → ~4.5 s. Same 12 strikes, same stages. More sparks per level (14 → 24). The crew's pace stays their levels.
- **Server floor:** `ForgeCheck.MIN_SECONDS_PER_STRIKE` 0.35 → 0.25, or a Lv. 5 forge would sit only 0.3 s above the refusal line and lag could reject it.
- **Prices and gates (Claude's):** I 250 after the Wakizashi, II 1,000 after the Katana, III 2,500 after the Sword Crafter, IV 5,000 after Anvil 3, V 10,000 after the Falchion. Pad `Strikes1-5` at plot (−31, 64), left of Anvil 1's hearth (checked clear).
- **Why these prices:** forging is ~11 s (measured median 10.8 s) of a ~100 s sword, so Lv. 5 saves ~5 s a sword and pays back slowly. In `economy_model`, a first try at 300 / 1,500 / 5,000 / 12,000 / 30,000 pushed a casual picker's Zanbatō from 8 h 28 to 8 h 35; the cheaper set leaves it at 8 h 27. A casual picker buys them at 10 min, 23 min, 1 h 29, 1 h 44 and 4 h 41.
- **Sim:** `ExportBalance` exports `strikePace`; `rules.strike_pace`; the engine's forge visit and `economy_model`'s sword time scale with it (the whole measured visit, a ponytail). Re-exported; `test_parity` + `test_engine` 32/32. SaveSpec 114/114, ForgeCheckSpec passed, every upgrade has a pad and known gates.

**To test in Play:** buy Faster Strikes I on the pad left of Anvil 1 (after the Wakizashi); the hammer swings and the blade slides a little faster with more sparks; at higher levels (set `Bought_Strikes1-5` on your plot) a full 12-strike forge still saves and grades 100% (no refusal), and an early Finish at 9 still works.
