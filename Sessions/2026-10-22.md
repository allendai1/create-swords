## Etching → polishing: lag and no visible polish

Allen: "i notice that it lags when i go from etching -> polishing. the look also stays the same. is this intended?"

- **The look:** both etch skins cover the whole blade face, so polish never showed on an etched sword.
  - Allen chose "Etch gets a sheen": the etch's dark body brightens at each polish level (Eclipse up to 0.3 toward (150, 190, 230), Flame up to 0.6).
  - The colour comes from `SwordMeshCache.etchBody`, which the etching show uses too.
  - The 6 Flame bakes at levels 1 and 2 were re-baked.
- **The lag:** crossing a polish level at the bench rebuilt the Eclipse image in one frame (about 190 ms on the katana).
  - Now the bench fetches it in the background, spread over frames, and keeps the current etch until it's ready.
  - It also holds its etch 5 s after leaving, so the saved sword's art doesn't rebuild it.
- **The bake tool now uses a fresh copy of Shared.** Edit's `require` served the old `SwordMeshCache`, which would have made the re-bake miss the change.
- **Backups:** `Legacy.*_beforeSheen` (SwordMeshCache, EtchCanvas, PolishController, EtchPlayback).
- **Checks:**
