**Juice**
- Every hit now throws sparks off the struck edge, plays a clang and gives a short camera shake. All three scale with the hammer (`JUICE` table at the top of AnvilController):

  | Hammer | Sparks | Clang pitch | Shake |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Heavy | 22 | 0.85 | 0.05 studs |
  | Normal | 12 | 1.0 | 0.03 studs |
  | Fine | 5 | 1.25 | 0.012 studs |

- Sparks: one ParticleEmitter on a Terrain attachment, burst with `Emit()` at the hit point and leaning outward from the edge. Uses the built-in `sparkles_main` texture stretched into streaks. (`fire_sparks_main` renders invisible.)
- Clang: free *Anvil Hits Ringing Metal Clinks Blacksmith 1* by ProSoundEffects (asset 9125361455), stored as `Workspace.Smithy.Anvil.Clang`. Each hit plays a clone with ±5% pitch variation, so fast hits overlap instead of cutting each other off.
- Shake: decays over 0.15 s, then the camera returns exactly to its forge position (verified, 0 drift).
- Playtested with no errors: sparks render, the clang plays at Heavy pitch (0.88), and the camera settles.

**Smooth blade (EditableMesh)**
- The 40 stacked slices are replaced by one `EditableMesh` blade (`Workspace.Blade.BladeMesh`, client-side). Its stations are the hilt end, each column centre and the tip end, with separate vertices for the top, bottom and side faces. It's rebuilt when the blueprint changes; after each hit only the vertices move.
- Verified in playtest:
  - dents are smooth curves and match the panel outline
  - the Trace short ingot renders at the hilt end
  - switching blueprints rebuilds the mesh
  - there's one blade object (no leak) and no errors
- Before launch, check Roblox's current rules for EditableMesh in published games. If it's blocked, switch to wedge slices (only the blade-drawing code changes).
- The mesh opens the door to bevelled edges and a raised spine (the open "shaping depth" question).

**Sharp tips**
- The blade ends used to copy the last column's width, so a pointed blueprint ended in a tiny flat rectangle. Both ends now continue the slope of the last two columns (half a column further, clamped 0–1), so a narrowing tip closes to a real point while the blank keeps its square end. Applied to the 3D mesh and the panel outline.
- Verified: the Shortsword at 8/8 in Clicks mode ends in a sharp point, with no errors.
- The point's *shape* is still the rounded "bullet" curve from Blueprints (`pointed(..., tipStart)` with exponent 1.6). It can be made straighter or longer per blueprint if wanted.

**Hot metal without the glow**
- Hot metal now uses a flat orange (SmoothPlastic, cooling from bright orange to dark red) instead of Neon, because the bloom hid the outline. The trace guide is plain white plastic too. Verified in playtest: edges and the Points circles read clearly, with no errors.
- Bring back a subtle glow in the art pass (e.g. a faint SurfaceLight or emissive texture rather than Neon).

**Accent lines on finished swords**
- Decided: the lines appear automatically on Finish, with no extra step.
- Blueprints can have a `fuller = { from, to, width }`. The Shortsword has one (4%–62% of the length); the Dagger has bevel lines only.

  - bevel lines at 60% of each edge's width, meeting at the point
  - the fuller with tapered ends, kept inside whatever outline the player made
- Finish now shows the finished sword for 2.5 s: cooled to iron, lines on, the result banner up, input paused. Then the next blank appears. Switching mode or blueprint cancels the showcase.

**Sharpened edges**
- The blade mesh now has a real cross-section: a full-thickness spine between the bevel lines (60% of each edge's width), with bevels sloping down to a thin edge (15% of the spine's thickness). The end caps are hexagons.
- While shaping, the bevel is light (`SHAPING_SHARPNESS = 0.35`). On Finish the edges grind to fully sharp over 0.5 s during the showcase, then reset with the next blank.
- Verified on a finished Shortsword: the upper bevel catches the light, the lower bevel is shaded, the point shines, the fuller still shows, with no errors. The real bevel now does most of the bevel lines' job; the overlay lines are barely noticeable on top of it.
- Tuning knobs at the top of AnvilController: `EDGE_RATIO`, `SHAPING_SHARPNESS`, `GRIND_TIME`, `BEVEL`.
