## Sword mesh pipeline spike

Allen: "Go ahead with the spike for the sword mesh pipeline: test publishing an EditableMesh as a mesh asset, vertex colours, UVs, Color tint and bounds."

- **Publishing from Studio is blocked:** `CreateAssetAsync` reports "not available yet". The fallback works:
  1. dump the mesh from Edit to a local receiver;
  2. convert it to glb with Python;
  3. upload it through Open Cloud;
  4. `InsertService:LoadAsset`.

  Approval took about 10 s.
- **Fidelity:** compared face by face against the live katana blade and its etch overlay. Positions, vertex colours, UVs and winding all match exactly (worst 0). `Color` still tints.
- **Two corrections found:**
  - The glTF import turns meshes 180° about Y, so the exporter negates x and z.
  - Uploaded meshes are re-centred on their bounds. The overlay needs `Offset` (1.7452, 0, −0.1341); blades need none, thanks to their bounds triangle.

## Sword mesh pipeline built

Allen: "Go ahead and build the bake tool, bake the swords and switch SwordMeshCache to baked-first."

- **`ServerStorage.Builders.bake_sword_meshes`** with the vault helper `Development/sword_mesh_bake.py`, which replaces the two spike scripts. All 27 shapes are baked into `ReplicatedStorage.SwordMeshes`. Each upload was approved; the whole bake took about 9 minutes.
- **The helper centres every bake** on the live mesh's origin with a degenerate bounds-mirroring triangle, so no `Offset` is needed and no caller changed.
- **`SwordMeshCache` is baked-first**, keyed on `Version`, falling back to live with one warning. Backup `Legacy.SwordMeshCache_beforeBaked`.

## One etch per sword

Allen: "i noticed that once i've etched something, im still able to etch it?"

- **Cause:** `EtchService` overwrote `sword.etch` without checking, and the sword picker let etched swords be picked.
- **Fix:**
  - `EtchService` returns false for an etched sword.
  - `EtchingTableController` marks etched swords' cards `locked` (dimmed, can't be picked), as the Polishing Station does for fully polished swords.
  - Backups: `Legacy.EtchService_beforeEtchOnce` and `Legacy.EtchingTableController_beforeEtchOnce`.
- Both compile. Not checked in Play.
