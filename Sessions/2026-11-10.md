## Blades can have holes

Allen asked why the new silhouettes' holes (S05's slot, Eclipse Fang's moon) couldn't be real holes, then: "so the gap is how we implemented, not on roblox then? that means it should be fixable no?" Yes: the limit was our profile format (one spine and one edge per station), not Roblox. Built in Edit.

- **`Shared/BladeCore`:** a profile's refined stage may carry `hole = { { u, a, b }, ... }`, the hole's sides as fractions across the blade from the spine (0) to the edge (1), closing to a == b at both ends. Stations carry `holeC` / `holeW` (`HOLE_PARAMS`); the earlier stages hold it closed on its centre line, so it opens on the tip stage's strike that reaches it. Only blades with a hole get the params and a block of 16 extra vertices per station (`HOLE`) after the caps: the top and bottom faces are drawn through the hole's two sides, with walls, and the ridge crease kept on each side. `hasHole(blade)`; `vertexCount` / `buildIndex` take `holed`; `stationOfVertex` / `ringOfVertex` answer for the hole vertices; `validateProfile` checks the hole opens, keeps 5% of steel each side and doesn't reach the base or point. One hole per blade (a second needs a second block).
- **`BladeView`, `BladeProbe`:** pass `hasHole` through.
- **`BladeEtch`:** a canvas is drawn opaque, so the etch skin itself stops at the hole: each row of a holed blade's overlay and Flame skin has every column twice, clamped to the hole's two sides, and the span between them isn't drawn (`splitAtHole`); overlay rows run closer over the hole. The Flame skin on a holed blade caps its rows so it stays under EditableMesh's 20k-triangle limit. The polish sheen uses the same overlay, so it stops there too.
- **Every existing blade is unchanged:** all 35 blueprints give the same vertices, triangles and positions in every forging state as before (compared against the backup), so no bake goes stale.
- **Tried on two test blades (not added to the game):** a longsword with an S05-style slot (29 stations open) and a 192-station zanbatō with a round hole across its ridge (11 open). Both pass `validateProfile` (hole, mesh outward, no snap, 3 grinding passes). Rendered in Edit plain, with the stroked overlay and with Flame: the holes go through cleanly and both skins stop at the rim. Round holes need more stations (the moon used 192).
- **Checked (Edit):** the four modules and SwordMeshCache load from a fresh copy of Shared; BladeCoreSpec 169 passed / 182 failed, the same failures as the backup's run (none new; they are blades without golden data); ForgeCheckSpec passed; ShapingRulesSpec 28/28. Allen (with a photo of a katana, the groove circled): "I want to Add a bō-hi (fuller) to our katana—the long, shallow recessed groove running along the blade near its spine ...
