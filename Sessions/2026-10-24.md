### What changed in the game

- **Ore box: spin as often as you like.** The lever is off only while a roll plays (supersedes 2026-10-22's "collect before rolling again"). Pulling it with ore still waiting puts that ore into your hands first (as many as fit; a NEW card for a first-time metal); what doesn't fit is rolled over. `ForgeService`.
- **Ore box: Merge Ore Slots (a rebirth for the roller).** With Ore Slots 2–6 bought, their pad spot becomes **Merge Ore Slots** ("Luck ×1.5", button "Merge"). Merging is free: Slots 2–6 go back to unbought (slot 1, coins and everything else stay), **luck ×(1 + 0.5 × merges)** on top of Lucky Roller, and the Ore Slot pads cost **×1.5 per merge** (Slot 2: 75 → 112 → 168; Slot 6: 4,000 → 6,000 → 9,000). Repeatable. Waiting ore goes to your hands first; with no room for it the merge is refused ("Your hands are full"). The Merge button waits 1 s after the pad appears under you, so mashing Buy through Ore Slot 6 can't merge by accident. The Ingot Rack, Lucky Roller I and Delivery Cart pads (after Ore Slot 2) stay available after a merge; the tutorial counts as done; "Workshop open!" doesn't replay. Saved as `merges`.
- **Luck past the old ceiling.** The roll used to break above luck ~2.24 (Common went negative). Now luck multiplies the rarer odds from the rarest down, each tier capped by what's left while every tier below keeps at least 10%: identical below 2.24, and each merge keeps raising rare ore. At Lucky III: Legendary 1.7%, then 2.6% / 3.5% / 4.4% after 1 / 2 / 3 merges; Common and Uncommon never drop under 10%. `Materials.roll`.
- **Deliveries: choose the swords.** Accepting an order opens a **packing page** like Create Pottery!'s: order chips (Bulk/Special, town, **Pin**), what it wants, "Tap swords to pack them", one crate slot per sword, your fitting swords as cards (what the cart pays for each, art, name, metal chip), and **Auto-Fill** (cheapest first, keeping your best for the shop), **Cancel Order**, **Fill the Crate** (veiled until every slot is full). Tap a card to pack it, a slot to take it back. Swords leave your inventory only when the crate is filled (one call, checked in full on the server). The trip tracker stays below. Half-packed crates from old saves show as locked full slots; Cancel returns them.
- **The star/level pill is gone from the HUD**; the reputation shows as "Rank N" in the Deliveries header, as before.
- **The pinned order ("2× Tanto 0/2") is off by default**: Pin on the packing page or the active order card turns it on for the session. No plate on it any more.
- **Orders: random gaps.** A new mail order arrives **3 to 10 minutes** after the last (uniform, a fresh gap per order), and the mail no longer says "New order in m:ss". An empty inbox says "No mail".
- **Left HUD: just the icons.** Menu buttons, the coin and gem counters and the pinned order have no plate or border; the numbers have a dark outline; icons 30 → 38 px. A gamepad's selection still shows an amber edge. **The gem shop button is the House icon.**

- **Toolbox:** a sword slot says the blade ("Katana") with the metal under it in small text, tinted by its rarity; the count shows only from 2×.

### How it was built

Three workflows, all in Edit:
1. **Scouts:** seven read-only agents mapped every item in the live scripts (root causes for the two crafter bugs, the 8-stud junction, the shelf and rack measurements, the watermark).
2. **Build:** five workstreams in parallel (ore box, deliveries, UI text, stations, map and metals) and the employee work as a three-step chain (crafter fixes + the staff framework + Stocker → Cashier + Promoter → Etcher + Etching Table 2). Each step edited small anchored pieces of the shared scripts and backed up everything first.
3. **Review:** a reviewer per area diffed every changed script against its backup; a second agent per area tried to refute each finding and fixed what survived. 15 findings, all fixed: the accidental merge when mashing Buy; ore left hidden on a merged slot; the roll losing Uncommon at very high luck; the crew standing 4 studs off the rack (the second rack's hooks moved the model's box); the Etcher's empty hook filled by the crafter, stalling all three employees; "Rack Full!" staying lit after buying Rack 2; longsword guards clipping the rack posts (already true before); the furnace prompt showing while the crafter had it; the pinned pill counting swords you'd since sold; the pinned pill's plate; the crew tabs off the left edge on phones; the crew dot lit for an idle Stocker; two "Walk-ins" rows on the Promoter's page; stale `StyleTest_CFrame` values that would have pulled 77 moved parts back if `build_style_test` were rerun. I then made Finished Sword Rack 2's prompt go off with the rack's while the crafter uses it.
