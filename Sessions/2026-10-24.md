### What changed in the game

- **Ore box: spin as often as you like.** The lever is off only while a roll plays (supersedes 2026-10-22's "collect before rolling again"). Pulling it with ore still waiting puts that ore into your hands first (as many as fit; a NEW card for a first-time metal); what doesn't fit is rolled over. `ForgeService`.
- **Ore box: Merge Ore Slots (a rebirth for the roller).** With Ore Slots 2–6 bought, their pad spot becomes **Merge Ore Slots** ("Luck ×1.5", button "Merge"). Merging is free: Slots 2–6 go back to unbought (slot 1, coins and everything else stay), **luck ×(1 + 0.5 × merges)** on top of Lucky Roller, and the Ore Slot pads cost **×1.5 per merge** (Slot 2: 75 → 112 → 168; Slot 6: 4,000 → 6,000 → 9,000). Repeatable. Waiting ore goes to your hands first; with no room for it the merge is refused ("Your hands are full"). The Merge button waits 1 s after the pad appears under you, so mashing Buy through Ore Slot 6 can't merge by accident. The Ingot Rack, Lucky Roller I and Delivery Cart pads (after Ore Slot 2) stay available after a merge; the tutorial counts as done; "Workshop open!" doesn't replay. Saved as `merges`.
- **Luck past the old ceiling.** The roll used to break above luck ~2.24 (Common went negative). Now luck multiplies the rarer odds from the rarest down, each tier capped by what's left while every tier below keeps at least 10%: identical below 2.24, and each merge keeps raising rare ore. At Lucky III: Legendary 1.7%, then 2.6% / 3.5% / 4.4% after 1 / 2 / 3 merges; Common and Uncommon never drop under 10%. `Materials.roll`.
- **Deliveries: choose the swords.** Accepting an order opens a **packing page** like Create Pottery!'s: order chips (Bulk/Special, town, **Pin**), what it wants, "Tap swords to pack them", one crate slot per sword, your fitting swords as cards (what the cart pays for each, art, name, metal chip), and **Auto-Fill** (cheapest first, keeping your best for the shop), **Cancel Order**, **Fill the Crate** (veiled until every slot is full). Tap a card to pack it, a slot to take it back. Swords leave your inventory only when the crate is filled (one call, checked in full on the server). The trip tracker stays below. Half-packed crates from old saves show as locked full slots; Cancel returns them.
