# Session 2026-10-19

Allen requested a Japanese setting inspired by Demon Slayer's Swordsmith Village, with customers from any sword tradition. Through a question-by-question review, the following direction was approved and saved in [[Design/Game Design Document|Game Design Document]] and [[Design/Decisions Log|Decisions Log]].

- **Setting and tone:** Japanese mountain swordsmith village inspired by Swordsmith Village; cozy daily life with spectacular individual visitors. Village-wide events deferred.
- **Workshop:** Open-front Japanese workshop, visible forging and displays facing the path; expand one increasingly elaborate workshop.
- **Starting blueprints:** Tantō → wakizashi → katana, taught through local commissions that provide the pattern when each request begins.
- **Early customers:** Mostly locals plus an early special outsider hinting at the wider world.
- **Commissions:** In-person requests and letters from returning customers replace smartphone texts.
- **Materials:** Keep the random Ore Roller, redesigned for the village, and the full fantasy material variety available from the start.
- **Wider traditions:** Visitors introduce new sword traditions through a first commission with its pattern; buy additional blueprints within that tradition.
- **Customer arrivals:** Displayed stock influences regular visitors; reputation unlocks surprise special visitors.
- **Special requests:** Untimed commissions; special visitors return when the requested sword is ready.
- **Customer tastes:** Regular customers have flexible tastes; special commissions specify combinations.
- **Haggling:** Retain negotiation and the risk that a customer leaves when pushed for a higher offer.
- **Fittings:** Match each sword tradition; unlock through crafting milestones, without a later coin-purchase requirement.
- **Scope:** Craft, collect, display, and sell; no combat or testing area for now.
- **Automation:** Keep both cashier and apprentice smith as later upgrades.
- **Decoration:** Functional displays and facilities improve business; purely decorative items are cosmetic.
- **Relationships:** Repeat sales build familiarity; milestones unlock personal commission chains and rewards.
- **Character roster:** Original village regulars plus recognizable fictional and historical special visitors. Leave their coexistence unexplained.
- **Quality:** Keep visible percentages and a price link, without named quality tiers.
- **Forging:** Hit every circle; each hit improves quality. No time limit, timing windows, misses, or cooling countdown. Supersedes earlier timed-strike/retry discussion.
- **Grinding:** All circles gives 90%; optional freehand grinding improves toward 100%. Remove only excess metal, stop at the target, never reduce quality.
- **Hilt assembly:** Three untimed taps, without timing bonuses or penalties.
