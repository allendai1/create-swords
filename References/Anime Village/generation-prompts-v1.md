# Anime Village Generation Prompts

## Town v1

Output: town-v1.png
Input images: none.

Exact prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: wide cinematic 3D environment concept art for Sell Swords!, a cozy swordsmith town in a Roblox game.
Primary request: original Japanese mountain swordsmith village architecture and town inspired by the atmospheric rural settlements, handcraft and warm lantern-lit streets of the Demon Slayer anime. Beautiful anime background-art sensibility translated into polished stylized medium-to-high-poly game environment rendering, hand-painted material treatment, clear large forms, appealing shapes, strong readable silhouettes. Architecture is the subject. Original buildings, no named characters or franchise symbols.

Composition: spacious elevated three-quarter view looking along a gentle curved village street into a sheltered mountain valley. The camera is low enough to appreciate building facades and raised roofs, high enough to understand courtyards and the street layout. Six generous player swordsmith properties arranged three along each side of the central village lane. Show each property's space clearly; not tiny market stalls. Each property has connected L-shaped wings: a road-facing retail sword shop with wide welcoming entrance and broad windows; a deeper open-front roofed smithing pavilion opening onto a generous paved working courtyard; a visible patch of expansion lawn. Between buildings maintain roomy walking paths and gaps. Players should be able to imagine walking along the lane and seeing active workshops. The foreground properties are detailed, background properties slightly simplified with atmospheric perspective. A small central gathering bend with a noticeboard and a modest courier handcart under a tree, no huge civic monument.

Architectural language: cream lime-plaster walls over darker rough stone foundations, deep warm cedar timber framing, chunky structural posts, gently curved blue-charcoal ceramic tiled roofs, tasteful extended eaves and exposed rafters, broad lattice shop windows, sliding wood-and-paper partitions, short indigo noren curtains with simple abstract geometric crests, a few vermilion accents, warm amber paper lanterns. Roof height and courtyard openness keep workshops bright and welcoming. Visible details include chunky masonry forges glowing in the open workshops, simple rectangular anvils, and a small number of legible sword display silhouettes. Props must support architecture, not clutter routes. Repeated construction kit with gentle variations in roof heights, storefronts and age, so this reads as a coherent game town.

Landscape: a clear shallow stream borders one side of the lane, one short curved timber footbridge connects to a side path, small stone-lined water channels, grouped bamboo and mossy rocks around boundaries, one or two restrained flowering trees, weathered stone steps to an ambient hillside path. Surrounding tall green forested slopes and layered dramatic blue mountains, a distant narrow waterfall. The landscape frames the village and does not crowd into working courtyards. Soft high clouds and mountain mist. No ocean, no giant castle, no skyscrapers, no modern vehicles, no torii gates at every house.

Lighting: warm late-afternoon sunlight with long soft shadows and glowing lantern interiors; luminous cool teal-blue mountain shadows, gentle atmospheric haze, warm/cool contrast and richly colored anime-environment mood. Clear daytime visibility of the game routes and workspaces; no darkness hiding the buildings.
Materials: stylized sculpted wood, broad hand-painted wood grain and stone variation, carefully simplified plaster textures. Refined bevels and curved roof geometry, rich color harmony, charming scale, detailed enough to guide a Blender environment build, not photorealism or noisy realistic grunge.
Frame: landscape 16:9 cinematic environment concept, full village composition with architecture in the foreground, no UI, labels, text, watermark, measurement marks, characters posing, combat, or weapons as foreground hero objects.

## Architecture kit v1

Output: architecture-kit-v1.png
Input image: town-v1.png, used as an architecture/material style reference.

Exact prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: architecture design sheet and 3D environment asset concept board for a Roblox Japanese mountain swordsmith town.
Input image 1 is a STYLE AND ARCHITECTURE REFERENCE for the village we just created. Match its cream plaster, warm cedar frames, blue-charcoal curved tile roofs, indigo noren with simple abstract crests, amber lanterns, stone plinths, bright anime-inspired mountain-village warmth and hand-painted stylized materials. Make a NEW clear architecture sheet rather than another town panorama.

Primary request: original architecture inspired by the atmospheric rural swordsmith settlements of Demon Slayer, imagined as a coherent medium-to-high-poly stylized 3D game-building kit. Chunky readable structure, sculpted softened edges, elegant curved eaves, simplified painted material variation. Show usable rooms and open frontages, not tiny inaccessible dollhouse props. Practical visual modeling reference with carefully composed readable building studies.

Layout: wide landscape presentation board, warm pale-grey studio background, generous clean spacing, no scenery horizon. Three large three-quarter architectural studies across the upper two-thirds:
1) LEFT: road-facing storefront wing, one story, broad open entry, wood lattice shop windows on both sides, short indigo curtain, visible sparse sword displays and a low counter inside. Large welcoming openings and wide stone entrance, timber/plaster walls, charcoal curved gable tiles with pleasing upturned corners, exposed rafters and only a few lanterns.
2) CENTER: deeper open-front smithing pavilion, one story, a long generous roof span with only three chunky front posts creating two broad clear openings, deep working floor, a masonry forge on the back wall, one broad rectangular hornless anvil well forward with plentiful space around it, a wide fittings bench near the wall. Keep sightlines open, avoid post forests and rows of tiny props. Warm interior glow, stone and packed-earth floor. No noren hanging in front of the anvil.
3) RIGHT: connected L-shaped shop-and-workshop property study, show how the two wings join using a coherent roof junction, surrounding a generous open paved working courtyard with a clear entrance route and empty grass expansion reserve alongside. This is ONE connected evolving workshop property. Room for additional anvils and employee racks; do not crowd it with furniture. Show approximate game-friendly functional zoning visually without text.

Bottom third: one neat spaced row of LARGE isolated architecture detail modules resting on the same neutral board: a chunky cedar post with stone foot and beam bracket, a plaster-and-timber wall panel with a lattice window, a grey curved tiled eave corner and ridge-cap segment, a short indigo curtain on a timber lintel, one warm paper lantern on a simple wall bracket, and a short low stone-base timber boundary fence with an open gate. These are component studies rather than extra full buildings. Each distinct and easy to read, no exploded assembly arrows, no technical diagrams. Consistent scale within each row, not misleading huge lanterns beside tiny buildings.

Overall style: polished clean 3D game environment concept art with the warmth and composition of anime backgrounds, mild hand-built asymmetry, visible rounded edge geometry, broad painted woodgrain and soft plaster variation, restrained decorative detail. Warm natural studio sunlight, soft contact shadows, highly legible materials, no photorealistic grunge or dense carved patterns. Buildings fully in frame, no overlapping studies, lots of negative space separating elements. No labels, Japanese writing, captions, logos, watermarks, characters, modern vehicles, weapons as hero objects, temple complexes, giant towers, torii at every entrance, or duplicated standalone miniature roofs above individual crafting props.
