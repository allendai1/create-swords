Primary request: Design a COZY, CLEAR, UNCLUTTERED furnace ingot selection window. Do not add a confirm, craft, start, load or purchase button.

Above them in small unobtrusive dark brown text, exact labels "1. Choose an ingot" and "2. Back to the plot". UI itself is razor sharp, clean flat game UI, not a hand-painted illustration. Text must be large, spelled correctly, readable, with a friendly rounded sans-serif similar to Fredoka for title and Nunito for labels. Broad simple shapes and subtle drop shadows; realistic Roblox UI widgets, no ornamental fantasy frame, no planks, nails, filigree, scrolling parchment or wood-grain texture on the actual UI.

No prices, percentages, stats, checkboxes or fake locked items.
