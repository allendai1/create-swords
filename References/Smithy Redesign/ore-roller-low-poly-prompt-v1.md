## Exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: clean low-poly 3D game prop concept image for a Roblox Japanese swordsmith crafting game.
Primary request: a single ORE ROLLER STAND model with its SEPARATE shared lever beside it. This is an appealing practical modeling reference, intentionally low-poly, flat-shaded, simple chunky geometry and clear readable silhouette.

Subject: ONE squat square stone ore pedestal, assembled from a handful of large warm cream sandstone blocks on four little charcoal feet. On top is a shallow warm orange-brown wooden tray with low chunky rails and minimal dark iron corner brackets. A single large faceted cyan-blue ore crystal chunk sits solidly in a central dark iron ore seat. Crystal is visibly an irregular mineral lump with about 12–20 large planar faces, not a perfectly round sphere. Ore is the brightest focal point. Rails are low enough that the entire ore result is easy to see. The stone pedestal is modest in height, about knee-to-waist high for a game avatar. The wood has broad simple color variation, no busy grain or photorealistic textures.

Beside the stand, with an obvious gap and its own separate small base, ONE sturdy wooden lever with a dark iron hinge housing and a big vermilion-red grip. Lever is clearly a standalone reusable control, not attached to or repeated on the pedestal; one lever could control several duplicated ore stands. It looks mechanically movable and reachable, tilted slightly toward the player. No shared platform linking the lever and the stand. Do not add extra ore stands or extra levers.

Composition: landscape studio product render, one centered three-quarter front view at slightly elevated gameplay angle, whole pedestal and whole lever fully visible, generous margins. Neutral warm light-grey background and matching matte ground, soft contact shadows, bright clean neutral lighting. No environment, building, UI, labels or text. Clear material separation: cream stone, warm orange-brown timber, charcoal iron, red handle, cyan ore. Playful, welcoming low-poly game style, crisp flat planar surfaces, very small simple bevels on wooden rails only. Restrained geometry, easy to reproduce in Blender and import into Roblox.

Absolutely no roof, canopy, shrine frame, overhead beam, tall posts, hanging ornaments, slot machine face, wheels, conveyor, gears, furnace, fire, chimney, character, swords, ornate decorations, realistic grunge, detailed scratches, high-poly smooth surfaces, logos or watermark. This is a roofless modular ore display/roulette stand: the ore changes appearance when the shared lever is pulled.
