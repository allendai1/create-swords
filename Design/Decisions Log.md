## Roadmap brainstorming decisions — 2026-10-22

- **Wealth through relaxed swordmaking:** Allen chose sword discovery/crafting as the activity focus, relaxed successful crafting over precision-based quality, and becoming rich as the larger tycoon fantasy.
- **Permanent growth:** retain wealth and workshop progress, expanding through valuable swords, facilities and prestigious customers; no rebirth/prestige reset in the current plan. Late-game milestones and expansion limits remain open.
- **Value first, then scale:** personally make more valuable swords, automate familiar production, then personally pursue new designs and special commissions.
- **Mixed unlocks:** profits buy most upgrades and blueprints; milestone commissions introduce special designs and new sword traditions.
- **Paid training per sword shape:** personally craft the shape before spending coins to train employees in it. Training covers any permitted supplied material, not separate material-and-shape combinations. Ownership, prices, material permissions and finish choices remain undecided.
- **Trained smiths can match manual quality:** lower starter output progresses toward **100% forging quality** when fully trained, replacing the older 90% employee ceiling. The player's continuing focus is new discoveries and special commissions. Starting quality, curve/costs and recovery mechanics remain open; finisher polishing capability is separate.
- **No ongoing wages:** hiring, recipe training and worker upgrades are one-time purchases; idle employees do not drain coins.
- **Later capped offline earnings:** once online automation is established and balanced, permit limited workshop earnings while away, collected on return. Cap duration, rate, unlock, stock consumption and ore-attendant contribution remain open. This supersedes leaving offline earnings as an unselected inbox idea; courier offline behavior is separate.
- **Staged ore supply:** player-rolled and stocked ore initially; an ore attendant is a later upgrade using the existing roller and material odds. This supersedes the older exclusion of an ore attendant and extends the five initial professions with a later sixth.
- **First employee revised — sword crafter:** Allen selected a worker who operates the anvil, polishes and puts the sword in its rack. This supersedes the first-hire smith/finisher separation and makes the earlier five-role count provisional. Hilt assembly responsibility and input supply need clarification before finalizing the combined job; polishing remains on assembled swords.

These are design decisions, not implementation claims. See [[Design/Workshop Growth#Wealth and staged automation (confirmed 2026-10-22)]] and [[Development/Roadmap]]. Claude is working on roadmap sections 1–3; this discussion refines later progression.
