## The short version

- **Apprentice $900, hired at 14:01.** Its pad appears at **7:31, the moment Planter Shape ($200) is bought**. The Clay Shelf was bought two minutes earlier (5:35) and brought only Clay Shelf 2. One run can't rule out that both are needed.
- **Only 4 coin buys come before it, $630 in all.** Then a 6½-minute save with the pad in plain sight. He walked past six cheaper pads ($120–$600) to get it.
- **The Shop menu was never opened.** Every buy was a floor pad.
- The Apprentice cost **55% of everything earned by 15 min** ($900 of $1,642) and **1.4× all earlier buys combined**. It's a milestone by size (the biggest buy so far), not by distance.

## What a 15-minute Sword Crafter needs

In Pottery's proportions, with the sim's casual income (~3,200 by 15 min):
- **Price about 55% of 15-min income: roughly 1,500–2,000.** The engaged types earn that by about 5–10 min.
- **His gate has to fit inside what a casual player already buys by 15 min** (10 pads, ~2,600). Furnace 2 + Anvil 2 (6,200) can't stand in front of him.
- **Pottery's order does that: the hire first, the extra station after.** For us, the Sword Crafter would come with or before his anvil, and Furnace 2 would follow him, as Furnace 3 already does (Allen 2026-11-02: "the furnace is for the crafter").
- **Knock-ons:** the 2026-10-29 targets put the Katana at 15 min, Haru at 3:00 and the Stocker at 3:30. A 15-min Taise hires alongside the Katana. The Stocker (12,000, after him) and the full-rack wait need a look. Allen also wants the hire to "feel like a milestone" and upgrades not "too easy" (2026-10-30); Pottery keeps that by making the hire the biggest buy so far, shown early, a ~6-sale save.

**Simulated** ([[Development/Sim Runs/crafter-15min-2026-11-02]]): price alone doesn't do it (2,000 behind today's gate: casual 53 min). "Hire first" (Anvil 2 comes with him, gate Ingot Rack + Sword Rack) hires engaged players at 10–20 min. Casual players get him at 15 / 17 / 19 min (1,500 / 2,000 / 3,000) when they go for him once he stands, and at 22–45 min when they buy cheapest-first. With the sim's sit-back estimate, casual 8 h coins drop 17–19%; with players forging on they're +2%. Haru comes ~40–50 min later either way.

**Built 2026-11-02** (Allen: "Yes, build it"): the Sword Crafter costs 2,000 with Anvil 2, after the Ingot Rack and Sword Rack ([[Sessions/2026-11-02#The 15-minute Sword Crafter built]]). Buying him stays the player's choice (Allen: "players dont necessarily have to buy the swordcrafter"; no pull built, [[Design/Crafter Pull Options — 2026-11-02]]). Still open: whether players keep forging after the hire.
