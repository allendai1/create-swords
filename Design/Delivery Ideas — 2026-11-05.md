# Delivery Ideas — 2026-11-05

Allen ([[Sessions/2026-11-05#notes.md fix list]]): "remove the roof structure over the delivery bay - think of other ways to make delivery an important part of the game." The cart shelter is gone; these are ideas only, nothing built. Today the cart takes a packed order from the board, goes away for a trip (2:30 down to 1:10 with Speed) and pays 0.8x the swords' value; Capacity and Speed are bought in the Deliveries window ([[Design/Workshop Growth#Optional remote delivery orders]]).

Ranked, with what each costs to build:

1. **Deliveries pay what the shop can't.** Each order also brings one thing only deliveries give: gems on most trips, a rare-ore crate now and then, a hilt or etch colour only from big orders. Today a delivery is just a slower, cheaper sale, so nothing makes you want it. Small: reward tables in `Shared/Deliveries`, one line on the board card.
2. **Rush orders.** Every 10-15 min the board shows one gold order: a named client, a short timer, 1.5-2x pay, a specific blade or metal. It's a reason to check the board and keep a spare sword back. Medium: a new offer type and its timer.
3. **The cart on the street.** The cart leaves through the bay gate, drives down the street to the west gate and comes back, so everyone sees deliveries go out and the street feels busy (the bustle Allen asked for). Medium: a path along the road like the rickshaws'.
4. **Regular clients with a story.** Three or four regulars (the dojo, the town guard, a merchant) whose orders get bigger as you fill them, each with a level and a thank-you letter in the mail. It gives the board a progression of its own. Medium-large: client data, levels and letters.
5. **The overflow outlet.** When the shop shelves are full, the Stocker (or you) can drop the cheapest swords into a standing "bulk" order that the cart takes on its next trip. Commons are worth something again and the racks never jam. Small-medium: a bulk order the cart always has.
6. **Away earnings.** Later, when offline earnings are decided ([[Design/Decisions Log]]: "Later capped offline earnings"), the cart is what earns while you're away: trips keep running on the packed orders. This ties delivery to coming back. Large: needs the offline rules first.

Claude's pick to try first: 1 then 2. They make the board worth checking without new map work.
