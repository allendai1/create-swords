# UI Audit — 2026-11-07

Every ScreenGui in the place, screened one window at a time at phone size and measured for padding and overlap; the big windows again at desktop. Allen's ask: "Look at all of the UI in our game, look for padding issues where there's overlapping UI or UI that had no padding between them. Screen all of the UI in our game, and look at each element individually." Built in Edit, **not played**. Pictures: `Art/UI/ui-audit-1107/` (one per state, phone size; `*-before` / `*-after` for the three windows that changed).

## How it was done

- Studio Edit, `StarterGui.ShowDevelopmentGui` on, the Device Simulator on **iPhone 14** (viewport 749 × 388, the safe area of an 844 × 390 phone) and **HD 1080** (1919 × 1079) for the desktop pass. Studio reset to its default viewport afterwards, the flag off, the camera Fixed at FOV 70.
- A new Edit-time tool, **`ServerStorage.Builders.ui_audit`** (kept, next to `coplanar_check`): `show(path)` turns a window and its Dim on and sets its `Fit` UIScale the way its controller does (`UiFx.fit` references: Menu / Gem / Mail windows 650 × 570, Deliveries 900 × 556, Crew and the confirms 860 × 560, pickers 940 × 760 / 600 × 380, anvil 1000 × 560, polish 560 × 420, Collection 552 × 520, hotbar 549 × 520); `clone(template, list, n, texts)` fills a runtime list from its `Templates`; `measure(path)` reads every visible element's AbsolutePosition and AbsoluteSize (clipped by its scrolling frames) and reports: off screen, under the 58 px top bar (IgnoreGuiInset windows), buttons under 36 px, text under 14 px (TextSize × the UIScales, or TextBounds when scaled), text that doesn't fit its box, text ink under 5 px from its painted box, overlapping elements that aren't layers (Shadow / Fill / Pattern / Tint / EdgeArt / Bevel / Glow … are skipped), siblings touching (gap under 3 px), list layouts with no padding; `restore()` puts every value back from `UA_*` attributes and deletes the clones. Require a fresh clone of it each call (Luau caches modules).
- 26 states at phone size: HUD (menu, coins, gems, Mail button, hotbar with 5 slots, weather icon, Auto-Roll bar), Shop, Settings, Odds, Volume, Daily Reward (7 days), Store (4 gem upgrades), Mail list (4 letters), popup + quest board (3 slips), the open letter, Deliveries (2 offers), Crew roster (3 phone cards), Crew Stats page, Level-up confirm, Merge confirm, Collection (a section of 8), the regular and the compact picker (6 cards), anvil view, polish view, etch view, hilt seat + the secret-sword card, bag (14 slots), VIP banner + spotlight + shop notice. Desktop: Shop, Store, Mail, Crew, Deliveries, Collection, Settings, regular picker; the same eight on an iPad A16 (1179 × 819) and the smallest phones (Galaxy A06 705 × 338, iPhone 7 666 × 374).

Content padding top 20 → 15 and bottom 28 → 10, the list gap 18 → 11, the Tabs row 48 → 46, and the two tab buttons full height less 4 (they were 26 px tall on a phone, now 30). 2. **Deliveries window** ran **21 px past the bottom** (373 px tall at 0.7). `Content.Body` 430 → 372 (the Offers and the packing page are 58 design px shorter and scroll as before), the Content's bottom padding 14 → 8. The header's **Cancel order** and **Upgrade cart** moved 6 px right: at desktop (1.5×) the DELIVERIES title ran into Cancel by 3 px. Now 329 px, ending at 387. Picture: `delivery-phone-before.jpg` → `delivery-phone-after.jpg` (in Edit every strip state shows at once; in play one does). 3. **Collection** ran **10 px past the bottom** (the fixed 528 × 442 window at 0.795). `CollectionController`'s fit reference height 488 → 520, so a 388 px phone gets 0.746 and the window is 329 px tall, ending at 387; the layout is unchanged, the card names are TextScaled. ## Smallest phones: the 0.7 floor loses the window's bottom

Measured after the fixes, same method. On a **360-px-tall Android** (Samsung Galaxy A06 / A16 / S22 Ultra / S25 Ultra: the simulator's viewport is 705 × 338) every window sits at its 0.7 floor and, centred 29 px low, runs past the bottom: Shop 19 px (the last card's buttons), Store 24 (the fourth gem upgrade's Buy), Mail 18, Deliveries 24, Collection 14, Settings 14 (a scrolling page, so only its border). On an **iPhone 7** (666 × 374): Store 6, Deliveries 6, Shop 1; Mail, Crew, Collection, Settings, the compact picker and the confirms fit. The Crew window (301 px) and the confirms fit everywhere.

Why: with the 29 px offset the space under the top bar is 280 px on those phones, and a 0.7 floor keeps the 650 × 570 family at 319–329 px. Nothing in the instances fixes it; it needs one of: a lower floor for the windows (0.62 → 20 px text reads 12.4 px, under the 14 px rule), scrolling bodies with a fixed height, or ~45 design px shorter layouts. Allen's call; left as the top open UI item. The 2026-10-31 phone review measured at 844 × 390 and 667 × 375 only.

**2026-11-10:** the **Shop** is fixed by a shorter layout: 396 tall (was 456), ending at y 336 on the 705 × 338 phone, every Buy and gift on screen ([[Sessions/2026-11-10#Shop fits a 338-px-tall phone (old style)]]). Store, Mail, Deliveries, Collection and Settings still run off there.

**2026-11-10, later:** **Settings, Store, Mail and Deliveries** fixed the same way (each ≤ 396 design px, ending at y 336; the Deliveries packing page's pictures moved beside their titles). The **Book** needed nothing: `CollectionController` sizes it to the screen as it opens (y 326–337 there); only its Edit template runs off. The smallest-phones item is closed pending a Play check.
