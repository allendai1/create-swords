## 1. Decoy purchase and a Best Value label

Allen: "look into a decoy purchase in shop and a best value label. decoy meaning"

**What a decoy is:** a third option priced so it's clearly worse than the one you want people to buy, which makes that one look like a deal. The classic: small popcorn $3, medium $6.50, large $7. Nobody wants the medium; it exists so the large feels like a steal.

**Where the Shop stands now** (amount per Robux, prices from §11 of [[Design/Monetization Plan]]):

| Coins | 800 | 1,800 | 5,000 | 20,000 | 100,000 | 300,000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R$ | 9 | 19 | 49 | 149 | 449 | 999 |
| per R$ | 89 | 95 | 102 | 134 | 223 | **300** |

| Gems | 7 | 15 | 80 | 200 | 500 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R$ | 9 | 19 | 99 | 229 | 449 |
| per R$ | 0.78 | 0.79 | 0.81 | 0.87 | **1.11** |

The bigger packs already give more per Robux, so a true "Best Value" label is free to add: **built tonight** on the 300,000 Coins and 500 Gems cards and the new Automation Pack, picked from the live prices.

**Options for the decoy:**

| Option | What changes | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Honest tiers (recommended)** | Keep every pack fair; tag the best one; add a "+N% more" chip on each pack showing the bonus over the smallest | Same nudge toward bigger packs, nothing a parent or Roblox could call a trick. Most players are kids ([[mobile-first]]) |
| B. Gem decoy | 200 Gems 229 → 399 R$, so 500 Gems at 449 looks like a steal | Strongest push to 500; the 200 pack becomes a bad buy on purpose |
| C. Pass decoy | Price the Automation Pack (179) close to Auto Ore Roller alone (149) | Already how the pack works: three passes for 30 more than one |

Claude recommends **A**, plus C as it already stands. B works, but it's a pack that exists to be a bad deal, and the audience is young.

**Question:** A, B, or both?

**Decided 2026-11-02:** B (Allen: "Fuck your recommendation of going against decoy pricing. I want it."). 200 Gems is 399 R$ on Roblox; the not-enough-gems popup skips it. [[Sessions/2026-11-02#Decoy pricing]]

## 2. Showcase stands outside the shop

Allen: "Can have display only racks or displays outside the store that can pull customers in as well - for players to display their coolest swords"

**Proposal (Claude's defaults, all tuning knobs):**

- **Showcase Stands 1–3**, on the street side of the shop front beside the door (the Shop Promoter's side), one sword each, upright and lit. Bought on pads, one chain: 3,000 / 25,000 / 90,000 coins, the first after Display Table 1.
- **Display only:** never sold, no offers. Store and take the way the racks work: a + on an empty stand, tap a sword to take it ([[station-ui-rules]]).
- **They pull customers in:** each filled stand brings walk-ins **5% sooner**, **10%** if its sword is Epic or rarer, or etched and polished. Three good swords: 30% (the Shop Promoter gives 20–35%).
- **Other players see them**, so your best swords are on show to visitors walking the street.
- The sim's walk-in rate gets the same rule.

**Questions:** where (outside by the door, or along the street edge of the plot); how many (3?); and is "rarer sword pulls more" right, or should every filled stand pull the same?

**Built 2026-11-02** (Allen: "Yes build thhe showcase stands"): as proposed, but on the street paving left of the shop (plot x -29 / -33 / -37): the forecourt by the door is only ~3 studs deep under the roof. ![[Art/Showcase Stands/2026-11-02 from the street.jpg]] [[Sessions/2026-11-02#Showcase Stands]]
