### Shared ids

Three cues reuse another sound's id:

- `RollLand` = the anvil `Clang`
- `RollDone` = `Upgrade`
- `RollClimb` = `FurnaceReady`

`Sale` also covers three different moments: a sale, finishing an etch and finishing a polish.

## Could add

Ranked by how often the player hits the moment with no sound now. Each one goes in `Effects` (or `Alerts` only if it calls the player somewhere). Follow [[Design/QoL and Settings Ideas — 2026-10-31]]: never signal by sound alone.

### 1. Core-loop gaps (every sword)

**Status 2026-11-10:** 1-4 are in (furnace whoosh, crackle and sizzle; etch grind; holder clink), plus a shop door bell for customers coming and going ([[Sessions/2026-11-10#Core-loop sounds: furnace, etching, holders, door bell]]). This list's "In the game now" tables are older than that and older than CoinTick, TutorialDone, the UI clicks and the new roll chime.

1. **Furnace heating:** a fire whoosh when the ingot goes in, then a low roar loop while it heats. The furnace is silent until the ready ding.
2. **Etching:** a quiet scratch/engrave loop while the pattern is drawn on the sword (the polish loop's pattern).
3. **Hot metal out of the furnace:** a short sizzle when the glowing blank comes out onto the anvil.
4. **Sword onto the rack / counter:** a short metal clink when a sword is placed for sale.
5. **Own polish/etch finish sound:** a bright shing for "finished" so `Sale` only means coins.

### 2. Rewards and feedback

1. **Rarity-scaled roll finish:** keep `RollDone` for Common/Uncommon; a bigger stinger for Rare+ and a fanfare for Epic/Legendary. Today every finish sounds the same.
2. **Coins counting up:** a soft coin tick on the counter's "+N" (rate-capped like `Cues.GAP`).
3. **Letter / mail order completed:** a short fanfare when a quest sword is handed in.
4. **Tutorial step done:** a light chime per finished step.
5. **New metal / sword unlocked:** a one-off discovery sting.

### 3. Mail and alerts

1. **New letter arrives:** a paper/bell cue in `Alerts` (it calls you to the mailbox).
2. **Customer walks in:** a shop door bell, quiet and spatial.

### 4. Ambience

1. **Forge crackle:** a quiet spatial fire loop at the furnace, rolled off at ~30 studs.
2. **Crew at work:** quiet spatial hammer clangs from employees' anvils (reuse `Clang` at low volume, pitch varied).
3. **Village bed:** distant chatter/birds outside the smithy, lower under the roof as rain does.
4. **Other weather:** wind or thunder if those weather types get added.

### 5. UI (low priority)

1. **Button click / panel open:** one soft click shared by every button. The QoL note warns against UI sounds just to fill a slider, so add only if playtests feel dead.
2. **Robux purchase done:** a reward sting after a pass or product is granted.
3. **Can't afford:** a soft dull thud on a blocked buy, paired with the visible blocked state.
