# Japanese Village Concept Art

Four matching visual explorations for the Japanese mountain-village direction. Created with the built-in image generation tool. These are proposals, not approved models or changes to gameplay. Exact prompts: [[References/Japanese Village/generation-prompts-v1]].

## Larger player property — latest direction

![[References/Japanese Village/player-plot-spacious-v2.png]]

Allen requested larger individual plots with a storefront and a dedicated crafting area. This concept proposes a connected L-shaped building: road-facing retail frontage, a roofed crafting wing, broad courtyard, and expansion lawn. The increased space and functional separation are requested; the exact L-shaped plan and dimensions are proposals. The earlier six-shop map should be revised to allow these larger plots.

The generated roller resembles ore bins rather than one material per stand, and the anvil is tapered. Retain the approved shared lever/one output per stand and use the standalone rectangular anvil reference for modeling. Exact prompt: [[References/Japanese Village/player-plot-prompt-v2]].

## Forge

![[References/Japanese Village/forge-v1.png]]

Pale masonry, dark stone, timber detailing, glowing chamber, and two ingot trays. Separate front/side studies aid modeling. Slot count is an illustrated configuration, not a new starter-capacity decision. Keep trays entering the heated chamber.

## Anvil

![[References/Japanese Village/anvil-v1.png]]

Broad hornless rectangular striking block mounted on a reinforced timber base. Slightly overhead view and hammer detail. Simplify grain, pitting, and stone variation for the actual stylized game asset; check workstation height and blade clearance against avatars.

## Village map

![[References/Japanese Village/village-map-v1.png]]

Six open-front shops face a shared village lane and central gathering space. Grass around workshops provides room for expansion. Stream, entry bridge, bamboo, mountain ridges, and a waterfall establish the sheltered setting. This is a layout concept; traversal distances, plot dimensions, and spawn placement require a blockout.

## Overall feel

![[References/Japanese Village/game-feel-v1.png]]

An inviting evening smithy, active forging, colorful blades on display, a visiting knight, and local foot traffic. Warm amber workshop lighting contrasts with cool mountains and dusk.

## Review limitations

- The atmosphere image takes liberties: it gives the anvil a tapered/horn-like shape and puts fittings on the heated blade. Use the standalone rectangular anvil reference and the approved blade-before-hilt crafting order for implementation.
- The mood scene's extra background architecture is decorative exploration, not an expansion of the six-player map plan.
- Surface texture density is higher than needed for production Roblox assets. Preserve strong silhouettes and material contrasts while simplifying details.
- No live Studio changes. The browser prototype remains paused.
