- A: "I would like it to be based off swords that are in the shop being sold, and maybe a smaller percentage of the recent income". Also "Yes" to the shop selling displayed swords while away.
- Now (built 2026-11-11, not played): 20% of your last 15 played minutes' income per minute away, up to 2 / 3 / 4 h by cashier. The swords on display don't matter.
- New design: while you're away, customers buy the swords on display; those swords are gone when you come back. On top, a smaller share of recent income.
- Q: how fast do displayed swords sell while away: the normal pace (a customer every 45-75 s), or slower?
- Q: at what price: each sword's value, or the usual offer range (115-150%)?
- Q: the income share: 10% (half of today's 20%)?
