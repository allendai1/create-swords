## A 15-minute Sword Crafter (options, 2026-11-02)

Allen's target since 2026-11-02: the first Sword Crafter at about 15 min of casual play ([[Design/Pottery Playthrough 2026-11-02]]). Scenarios in `Development/sim/scenarios.py`; the second half in [[Development/Sim Runs/crafter-15min-goal-2026-11-02]] (same flags and seed, so baseline is shared):
- `c2k`: Sword Crafter 2,000, today's gate (Anvil 2 after Furnace 2 and both racks).
- `firstNk`: **hire first, as in Pottery.** Anvil 2 comes with him (no Anvil 2 pad); he waits only for the Ingot Rack and Sword Rack (740), and his pad stands as soon as they're bought. Furnace 2 stays an ordinary pad.
- `-goal`: players head for him once he stands, as Allen did for Pottery's Apprentice (he walked past six cheaper pads). Without it, every type buys the cheaper pads round him first.
- `-no-sit-back`: players keep forging after the hire. Sitting back is the sim's estimate (Allen 2026-10-29).

What it says:
- **The gate is the blocker, not the price.** At 2,000 behind Furnace 2 and Anvil 2 (6,200), casual players still hire at 53 min.
- **Hire first reaches 15 min only if players go for him.** The Ingot Rack and Sword Rack open his pad at about 13 min for a casual player. Then a 2,000 Crafter takes a 4½-minute save, about 60% of 15-min income and 4.3× the biggest buy before him. That's Pottery's proportions (55% and 4.5×). Left to buy cheapest-first, casual players hire at 22–45 min. In the game, that pull would have to come from a tutorial step, a pinned next goal or the pad itself.
- **Engaged players hire at 10–20 min in every hire-first option.** Today it's 59–85 min.
- **The coin drop is the sit-back estimate.** With sitting back on, an early hire costs casual players 17–19% of 8 h coins, and the Longsword comes ~35 min later, because they forge a quarter less from 15 min on. With forging on, casual coins are +2%, the Longsword holds at 1:47 and the Stocker comes ~1 h 15 sooner. Efficient players lose 8–9% either way (the Zanbatō ~20 min later); not separated yet.
- **Knock-ons:**
  - The Stocker comes sooner: casual by 35–55 min (1 h 15 forging on), engaged by 8–26 min.
  - **Haru (2nd crafter) comes later:** efficient 1:39 → 2:17, casual 3:57 → 4:43–4:55 (target 3:00); 3:40 for casual with forging on. Not separated yet.
  - Waits over 15 min don't change (casual 9).
- **Sanity check** (median casual, `first2k-goal`): Polishing Station, Ore Slot 2, Wall Rack 2, the Wakizashi, Ore Slot 3, Sword Rack, the Machete, Display Table 1, Ingot Rack (13.3), Sword Crafter (17.6), then Faster Strikes I and Heating I. That's what a player saving for him would do.
