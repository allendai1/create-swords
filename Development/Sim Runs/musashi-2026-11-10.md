## Reading it

- Every player opens the stall right after the Sword Crafter (they already know the Katana). Design 1 (600 coins) is bought within minutes, and every player has all 10 designs by 9–16 h.
- Casual, automator and grinder reach the Seraph sooner and earn 9–25% more at 20 h. Efficient, rusher and manager reach the Seraph 10–52 min later. Designs 8–10 (333k–490k) are worth more than the Serpent, Leviathan and Seraph, so the value-first types buy them before those blueprints.
- Deals: about 28 a player. Most of the coins they save come late, on Ore Slots 4–6, whose prices compound after each merge (a 25% deal on a 17M slot). Early deals are small (Heat 1, Sword Rack 2).
- Buy order checked for efficient and casual player 1:0. Design 1 comes right after the Crafter. Design 5 (375) waits for the cheapest-pad rule for efficient players, because it doesn't beat the Rapier (380). Deals land on pads the type was buying anyway.

## Approximated

- **The wall clock:** each 1 h day starts at a random minute of the hour, so a session sees the login stock plus one new stock. A stock is rolled when first seen, which is when the floor counts too.
- **Random draws:** the sim's own random numbers, with the game's odds. Stock and deal draws have no golden vectors. `musashi_percent` and `musashi_discounted` are parity-tested.
- **Checking the stall** costs no time. A buy there costs the menu time (3 s), with no walk to the stall.
- **Which pads count as standing:** the pads on offer at the moment of the stock. A gem pad counts only once its gems are in hand. Gem pads are discounted in gems.
- **Paying for designs:** always coins. No type spends gems on swords.
- **Noise:** no fresh-seed baseline-vs-baseline check was run.
